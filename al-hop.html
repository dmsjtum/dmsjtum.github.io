AL-HOP: Augmented-Lagrangian Horizon-Optimal Planning for Fast Constrained Trajectory Optimization

Miaomiao Dai, Zhongqiang Ren · Shanghai Jiao Tong University代淼淼，Zhongqiang Ren · 上海交通大学

Choose the duration while respecting the constraints that make a trajectory usable.在选择运动时长的同时，满足让轨迹真正可用的约束。
  • Discuss this research交流这项研究

Duration matters under constraints约束下，时长同样重要

A useful trajectory must do more than reach a goal quickly. It must respect actuator limits, state bounds, and obstacles. AL-HOP extends horizon selection to this constrained setting, optimizing the motion and its integer terminal index under a common cost.

一条可用轨迹不仅要尽快抵达目标，还必须满足执行器限制、状态边界和障碍约束。AL-HOP 将视界选择扩展到这一场景，在统一代价下优化运动及其整数终止时刻。

Changing the horizon also changes which constraints matter. A promising prediction from a local model therefore needs to be solved and checked under the original nonlinear dynamics.

视界改变时，起作用的约束也可能改变。因此，局部模型给出的有希望的预测，还需要在原始非线性动力学下求解并验证。

Predict, solve, verify预测、求解、验证

An augmented-Lagrangian formulation adds constraint information to the local costs while preserving the structure needed for HOP’s shared horizon evaluation. AL-HOP limits the proposed horizon change and runs a constrained nonlinear optimization at that candidate.

增广拉格朗日形式将约束信息纳入局部代价，同时保留 HOP 共享视界评估所需的结构。AL-HOP 控制候选视界的调整幅度，并在该视界上进行约束非线性优化。

A candidate is retained only when it is feasible and improves the original objective. This acceptance step is central: a fast horizon score alone is not evidence that a trajectory satisfies the constraints.

只有可行且改善原始目标函数的候选才会被保留。这个接受步骤很关键：快速得到一个视界评分，并不意味着轨迹已经满足约束。

Reliability and speed, measured separately分别看有效性与速度

The benchmark covers 100 problems across 10 systems, with 5 runs per problem and method. Every returned trajectory is checked against the same terminal-error, dynamics, and knot-constraint tolerances; timeouts count as failed attempts.

基准覆盖 10 类系统、100 个问题，每个方法对每题运行 5 次。返回轨迹采用相同的终端误差、动力学与节点约束容差进行后验检查，超时调用计为未通过。

Speed and quality. On the problems both methods solve, AL-HOP is 2.39×, 4.76×, and 3.34× faster than ALTRO, DIRCOL/IPOPT, and DIRCOL/SNOPT, while finding solutions with similar costs.

速度与质量。在双方都有效的问题上，AL-HOP 比 ALTRO、DIRCOL/IPOPT、DIRCOL/SNOPT 分别快 2.39、4.76、3.34 倍，同时获得代价相当的解。

A local solver, with explicit checks带有显式验证的局部求解器

AL-HOP remains a local nonlinear method. Validation checks the discretized trajectory under nonlinear replay, not continuous-time collision freedom between every pair of knots. The measured comparisons concern complete solvers with different discretizations and solver designs; the results should be read within that benchmark scope.

AL-HOP 仍是局部非线性方法。验证检查离散轨迹的非线性回放，并不保证任意两个离散节点之间的连续时间无碰撞。对照比较的是采用不同离散化与求解设计的完整求解器，结果应在该基准范围内理解。
