Control-Faithful World Models

Latent dynamics whose local responses drive Riccati feedback on frozen VLAs.让隐空间世界模型的局部响应足够准确，用来驱动冻结 VLA 上的 Riccati 反馈。

Miaomiao Dai · One-page research proposal · September 2026 · formerly “Flow2Funnel”一页研究计划 · 2026 年 9 月 · 原名 “Flow2Funnel”

Research question核心问题

A world model used for control is asked one thing: what happens if I nudge the action? Can we train latent world models so that this answer—their local response along a plan—is faithful, and use it to correct a frozen VLA’s plan at every control step?用于控制的世界模型，其实只被问一件事：把动作轻推一下，会发生什么？能否训练隐空间世界模型，让它沿计划的局部响应足够准确，并用这个响应在每个控制步修正冻结 VLA 的计划？

Why world models, and why faithfulness为什么要世界模型，为什么强调“可信”

Vision–language–action models plan well but execute blind: they emit an action chunk from one frame and run it open loop. When objects move or inference lags, the chunk goes stale. On DOMINO, a dynamic-manipulation benchmark, π0.5 succeeds on 44.8% of static tasks but on under 10% once the targets move [1].

视觉–语言–动作模型擅长出计划，执行时却是“闭眼”的：它根据单帧画面给出一段动作，然后开环执行。物体一动或推理一慢，这段动作就过时了。在动态操作基准 DOMINO 上，π0.5 在静态任务上成功率 44.8%，目标一旦运动就不到 10% [1]。

A world model is the natural fix: predict how the scene will evolve, then correct. But world models are trained and judged on how well they imagine the future. A controller needs something narrower and harder: the right local response to a small change of action—the A and B of the linearized dynamics. A model can roll out plausibly and still get this derivative wrong, and then feedback pushes the wrong way.

世界模型是自然的补救：先预测场景如何演化，再修正。但世界模型通常按“想象未来像不像”来训练和评价，而控制器需要的东西更窄也更难：动作微小改变时的局部响应，即线性化动力学里的 A 和 B。一个模型可以滚动预测得很像样，导数却是错的；这时反馈会往错的方向推。

The idea方法

  1. Latent state. Encode the recent camera and proprioception history into a compact state z: frozen visual features plus a small learned map.隐状态。把最近的相机画面与本体感知历史编码成紧凑的状态 z：冻结的视觉特征，加一个小的可学习映射。
  2. One world model. Learn zk+1 = F(zk, ak). Along the VLA’s plan its derivatives give Ak = ∂F/∂z and Bk = ∂F/∂a; the controller uses the model’s own linearization, not separate matrix heads.一个世界模型。学习 zk+1 = F(zk, ak)。沿 VLA 的计划求导即得 Ak = ∂F/∂z 与 Bk = ∂F/∂a；控制器直接用模型自身的线性化，不另设矩阵输出头。
  3. Riccati at every step. Re-solve a box-constrained LQR over the rest of the plan at each control step and add the bounded correction to the VLA’s action. The VLA stays frozen.每步 Riccati。每个控制步沿剩余计划重解一次带盒约束的 LQR，把有界修正加到 VLA 的动作上；VLA 本身保持冻结。

Training signal: push-and-see pairs. Rerun the same plan with a small push ±δ at step j, hold every later command fixed, and fit F so that its response matches the measured one at every later step k:训练信号：“推一下看看”的成对回放。同一计划重跑一遍，只在第 j 步施加 ±δ 的轻推、之后的命令全部锁定；让 F 的响应在之后每一步 k 都与实测一致：

Δyk / 2δ  ≈  C Ak−1 … Aj+1 Bj

plus predicting the unpushed rollout. No reward and no pixel reconstruction: the model is trained on exactly the quantity the controller consumes.再加上预测未推动的那条轨迹。不需要奖励，也不重建像素：模型被训练的，正是控制器要用的那个量。

Why this could work为什么可能有效

Pilot studies on simulated mechanisms point to where faithfulness matters. Re-solving at every step already forgives errors in the size of the response; it cannot forgive errors in its direction or in the scene’s memory—inertia, springs, a target that keeps moving. Those are exactly what a camera reveals and a single global model gets wrong, so they are where a learned, state-dependent Ak and Bk should pay off. In the same pilots, a correction head fitted to one VLA’s plans did not carry over to another VLA, while a model of the world’s response carried over better.

在仿真机构上的初步实验提示了“可信”在哪里要紧：每步重解已经能容忍响应大小的误差，却容忍不了响应方向的误差，也容忍不了场景的记忆（惯性、弹簧、持续运动的目标）。这些正是相机看得见、而单一全局模型会弄错的量，所以学出随状态变化的 Ak、Bk 应当在这里见效。同一批实验中，按一个 VLA 的计划拟合的修正头迁移不到另一个 VLA，而“世界如何响应”的模型迁移得更好。

These are pilot observations that shaped the design, not results.以上是影响设计的初步观察，不是结论。

How it will be tested如何检验

Benchmark. DOMINO [1]: 35 dual-arm tasks with moving targets and an official π0.5 checkpoint. The study uses a subset where π0.5 works on static scenes but fails once objects move, after reproducing the official baselines. The latency protocol of real-time chunking [2] and learned chunk correction [3] on Kinetix and LIBERO gives a second, directly comparable testbed.

基准。DOMINO [1]：35 个双臂任务，目标会运动，并提供官方 π0.5 权重。选取“静态会做、物体一动就失败”的任务子集，先复现官方基线再比较方法。另用实时分块 [2] 与学习式块修正 [3] 在 Kinetix 和 LIBERO 上的延迟评测协议，作为可直接对比的第二个测试床。

1 · Room1 · 余量With the true local response, Riccati must beat the best single global model by at least 10 points; otherwise the setting does not reward learning A and B, and the setting changes, not the claim.给出真实局部响应时，Riccati 必须比最好的单一全局模型高至少 10 个百分点；否则说明这个设定不奖励学 A、B，要换的是设定，而不是主张。
2 · Faithfulness2 · 可信On held-out pushes, the predicted response is within 20% at the 90th percentile and at least 20% better than a global model, without hurting open-loop prediction.在留出的轻推上，预测响应的误差 90 分位 ≤ 20%，且比全局模型小至少 20%，同时不损害开环预测。
3 · Closed loop3 · 闭环On held-out scenes and three seeds, beat the VLA alone, a global model, a shuffled latent, and a correction head that reads the same inputs.在留出场景、三个种子上，胜过单独的 VLA、全局模型、打乱的隐状态，以及读取相同输入的修正头。
4 · Reuse4 · 复用The same frozen model serves new targets, cost weights, action limits and latency—only Riccati’s inputs change—against a correction head that also reads the task.同一个冻结的模型服务新的目标、代价权重、动作上限与延迟（只改 Riccati 的输入），对照同样读取任务信息的修正头。

What it would add to world models对世界模型研究的意义

If it works, control-faithfulness becomes a measurable property of world models alongside rollout quality: paired pushes are a cheap probe for asking any action-conditioned world model—video world models included—whether it knows what an action does, and a training signal to fix it when it does not. The control side stays classical and transparent: one model, its derivatives, one Riccati pass.

如果成立，控制可信度就成为世界模型与“滚动预测质量”并列、可以测量的属性：成对轻推是一种廉价的探针，可以拿来检验任何动作条件的世界模型（包括视频世界模型）是否知道一个动作会带来什么；它答错时，同一信号又能用来训练纠正。控制部分保持经典、透明：一个模型、它的导数、一次 Riccati。

Status (September 2026). Simulated pilot mechanisms are built; the official π0.5 baselines are being reproduced on a DOMINO subset before any method comparison. An earlier version of this idea (Flow2Funnel) trained separate local heads per plan; its architecture is kept for reference.当前进展（2026 年 9 月）。仿真中的初步机构已搭好；正在 DOMINO 子集上复现官方 π0.5 基线，之后才做方法比较。本构想的早期版本（Flow2Funnel）为每个计划单独训练局部输出头，其架构图保留备查。
