Research Experience
HOP: Horizon-Optimal Trajectory Planning
Jul 2025 – Feb 2026
Advisor: Prof. Zhongqiang Ren, Shanghai Jiao Tong University · RSS 2026 talk, undergraduate first author
• Proposed HOP, a new algorithm that makes LQR and DDP horizon-optimal: it optimizes the trajectory and its horizon

together instead of fixing the horizon in advance.

• Proved the Riccati recursion is a linear fractional transformation, so all candidate horizons share one computation,

O(N 2 ) → O(N ) even when time-varying; HOP-DDP handles nonlinear systems, up to 40× faster than brute force.
• Designed and iterated the experimental evaluation across cartpole, Segway, and quadrotor systems.

AL-HOP: Constrained Horizon-Optimal Planning
Mar 2026 – present
Advisor: Prof. Zhongqiang Ren, Shanghai Jiao Tong University · first-author manuscript in submission to ICRA 2027
• Extends HOP to hard state–control constraints (obstacles, actuator limits) with an augmented-Lagrangian formulation

that keeps the one-sweep horizon prediction.
• Produced solutions valid under the full nonlinear dynamics on 100/100 benchmark problems across 10 dynamical
systems, vs. 79, 71, and 55 for ALTRO and direct collocation (IPOPT, SNOPT); where both were valid, baselines needed
1.6–3.1× the solve time.
PhysLA: Transferring Physical Supervision to Latent-Action VLA Pretraining
Researcher, Shanghai Jiao Tong University · second author, under review at ICLR 2027

May 2026 – Sep 2026

• Latent-action pretraining supervises contact and force only indirectly, and tactile or torque sensing covers only a small

slice of video. PhysLA learns physical targets where sensors exist, infers them from causal RGB elsewhere, and uses them
with visual prediction to supervise latent actions — no tactile or torque sensing at deployment.
• Designed two of its three stages and co-implemented them: learning horizon-structured physical targets from pressure,
tactile, and torque measurements, and transferring them to sensor-free video via causal RGB.
• Outperforms VLA-JEPA, the strongest latent-action baseline tested, on LIBERO and every SimplerEnv task group, and
beats both VLA-JEPA and π0.5 on each of five real-world manipulation tasks.
Voice-Interactive Closed-Loop VLA Manipulation (B.E. thesis)
Feb 2026 – Aug 2026
Advisors: Prof. Hesheng Wang & Prof. Yunlong Guo, Shanghai Jiao Tong University · group leader, team of 5
• Collected and trained on real robot data: teleoperated 316 episodes across 11 tabletop tasks on a low-cost SO-101,

then fine-tuned π0.5 on them — LoRA on the 2B vision–language backbone, full training of the 300M action expert.

• Closed the execution loop: a VLA emits open-loop action chunks and never checks the result, so I added a lower-

rate visual supervisor over progress and terminal state that triggers stop, return, re-observation, or a bounded retry
(terminal-verification F1 0.842).
• Reached 70% end-to-end success over 11 tasks (85% pick, 75% placement) with zero safety incidents; unseen object–target
compositions are reported separately as an open condition, and built the voice-interaction and monitoring interface.
