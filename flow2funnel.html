Flow2Funnel: Feedback-Complete Action Heads for Vision–Language–Action Policies

A policy commits to a whole chunk of actions and runs it blind. This is a proposal about the gap in between. 策略一次定下一整块动作，然后闭着眼睛跑完。这份提案讲的是中间那段空白。

Proposal · solo · looking for an advisor 研究提案 · 独立进行 · 正在寻找导师

Where it stands目前进展

The head trains and integrates into a frozen open-source backbone with no measurable side effect. The mechanism is validated end to end in a contact-rich setting, against arms that include an oracle upper bound and the obvious cheap alternative. The decisive experiment on the real policy is running now, on the one GPU this has.

这个头能训出来，挂进一个冻结的开源主干时对策略没有可测的副作用。机制已在富接触场景端到端验证过，对照臂里既有 oracle 上界，也有那个显而易见的廉价替代。真策略上的决定性实验正在跑——在这个项目仅有的那块 GPU 上。
