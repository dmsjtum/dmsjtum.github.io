I am a Master of Science in Robotics student at the University of Pennsylvania, interested in the space where optimal control meets robot learning. Much of my work so far is in trajectory optimization, on a question standard planners skip: how long should a motion take? Conventional solvers fix the duration up front and re-solve for every guess. My work makes that choice part of the solve itself.

I also deploy vision-language-action models on real hardware, which is a useful corrective. The distance between a clean theoretical result and an arm that actually picks up the right object is where most of the interesting problems turn out to live.

My current project asks whether measured touch can teach a visual representation what vision alone does not: the model trains against real pressure and force signals, then deploys with nothing but RGB.

我目前在宾夕法尼亚大学攻读机器人学硕士（MSR），研究兴趣在最优控制与机器人学习的交界处。我目前的多数工作在轨迹优化上，关注的是大多数规划器会跳过的一个问题：一段运动究竟应该持续多久？传统求解器把时长当作提前给定的参数，每换一个猜测就要重解一次。我的工作把这个选择本身变成求解的一部分。

我也在真实硬件上部署视觉-语言-动作模型。这件事很能治人：从一个干净的理论结果，到一条真的能把正确物体抓起来的机械臂，中间的距离才是大部分有意思的问题所在。

我目前的项目在问：实测的触觉能不能教会视觉表征一些仅靠视觉学不到的东西——训练时以真实的压力与力信号为监督，部署时只看 RGB。

§02Papers论文

HOP: Fast Differential Dynamic Programming for Horizon-Optimal Trajectory Planning

Miaomiao Dai, Zhongqiang Ren (advisor), SJTU（导师），上海交大

RSS 2026 · Robotics: Science and Systems

Undergraduate first author · presented the talk 本科生第一作者 · 本人作大会报告

  • Idea. Standard planners fix the task duration up front; HOP selects the optimal horizon inside a single solve.
  • Theory. A closed-form result, proved, scores every candidate horizon in one backward sweep — horizon search drops from O(N²) to O(N).
  • Result. Up to 40× faster than brute-force search, with the identical selected horizon and cost.
  • 思路：标准规划器把任务时长当作提前给定；HOP 在单次求解内直接选出最优视界。
  • 理论：提出并证明了一个闭式结果：单次反向扫描评估所有候选视界，视界搜索由 O(N²) 降到 O(N)。
  • 结果：比暴力搜索快至多 40 倍，且选出的视界与代价完全一致。

AL-HOP: Augmented-Lagrangian Horizon-Optimal Planning for Fast Constrained Trajectory Optimization

Miaomiao Dai, Zhongqiang Ren (advisor), SJTU（导师），上海交大

In submission · ICRA 2027投稿中 · ICRA 2027

First author 第一作者

  • Idea. Extends HOP to hard state and control constraints — obstacles, actuator limits — while keeping the one-sweep horizon prediction.
  • Validity. Solutions valid under the full nonlinear dynamics on 100/100 benchmarks across 10 systems, versus 79 / 71 / 55 for ALTRO, IPOPT, and SNOPT.
  • Speed. Where both were valid, the baselines took 1.6–3.1× the solve time.
  • 思路：将 HOP 推广到硬状态与控制约束（障碍物、执行器极限），同时保留单次扫描的视界预测。
  • 有效性：在 10 类动力学系统的 100/100 个基准问题上给出满足完整非线性动力学的有效解；ALTRO / IPOPT / SNOPT 分别为 79 / 71 / 55。
  • 速度：在双方均有效的问题上，基线耗时为 AL-HOP 的 1.6–3.1 倍。

Touch Without Touch: Measured-Touch Supervision for Visual Representations

Advisors: Prof. Hesheng Wang, SJTU IRMV Lab · Prof. Lin Shao, RoboScience 导师：王贺升 教授（上海交大 IRMV 实验室）· 邵林 教授（RoboScience）

Ongoing research · 2026 — 进行中的研究 · 2026 —

Lead author 主要负责人

  • Question. Can measured pressure, force, and contact teach a visual representation what visual self-supervision alone does not?
  • System. JEPA-style latent prediction over five heterogeneous touch datasets; deployment stays RGB-only, no tactile sensor.
  • Rigor. Every comparison and falsification criterion preregistered before training.
  • 问题：实测的压力、力与接触，能否教会视觉表征仅靠视觉自监督学不到的东西？
  • 系统：JEPA 式潜空间预测，统一五个异构触觉数据集；部署时只看 RGB，无需触觉传感器。
  • 严谨性：所有对照与证伪判据均在训练开始前预注册。

A Voice-Interactive Closed-Loop VLA System for Low-Cost Tabletop Manipulation

Miaomiao Dai, Siyuan Li, Weiyi Wang, Xiayang Jin, Yijie Liao · Advisors: Prof. Hesheng Wang & Prof. Yunlong Guo · 导师：王贺升、郭云龙 教授

B.E. Thesis · Shanghai Jiao Tong University · 2026 本科毕业设计 · 上海交通大学 · 2026

Project lead · team of 5 项目负责人 · 五人团队

  • Data and policy. Teleoperated 316 episodes across 11 tabletop tasks on a low-cost SO-101, then fine-tuned π0.5 on them — LoRA on the 2B vision-language backbone, full training of the 300M action expert.
  • Closing the loop. A VLA emits open-loop action chunks and never checks the result. A lower-rate visual supervisor watches progress and terminal state, and triggers stop, return, re-observation, or a bounded retry — F1 0.842 on terminal verification.
  • Evaluation. 70% end-to-end over 11 tasks (85% pick, 75% placement). Unseen object–target compositions are reported separately as an open condition, not as success.
  • 数据与策略：在低成本 SO-101 上遥操作采集 11 类桌面任务共 316 条 episode，据此微调 π0.5：2B 视觉语言主干走 LoRA，300M action expert 全量训练。
  • 把回路闭上：VLA 输出开环动作块、从不核验结果。低频视觉监督器持续判断进度与终态，触发停止、返回、重新观测或有界重试——终态判定 F1 0.842。
  • 评测：11 类任务端到端成功率 70%（抓取 85%、放置 75%）。未见过的「物体–目标」组合单独作为开放条件报告，不计入成功率。
