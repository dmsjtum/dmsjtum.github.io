HOP: Fast Differential Dynamic Programming for Horizon-Optimal Trajectory Planning

Miaomiao Dai, Zhongqiang Ren

Accepted · RSS 2026 · First author as an undergraduate已录用 · RSS 2026 · 本科期间第一作者

  • Idea. Optimize the trajectory and its horizon together.
  • Method. Shared LQ computations reduce horizon evaluation from O(N²) to O(N) for a fixed state dimension.
  • Result. Up to 40× faster overall solves than the brute-force baseline in the reported experiments.
  • 思路：同时优化轨迹与运动视界。
  • 方法：共享线性二次模型的计算；固定状态维度时，视界评估由 O(N²) 降至 O(N)。
  • 结果：论文实验中，相较暴力搜索基线整体求解最高加速 40 倍。

AL-HOP: Augmented-Lagrangian Horizon-Optimal Planning for Fast Constrained Trajectory Optimization

Miaomiao Dai · First author · Advisor: Zhongqiang Ren代淼淼 · 第一作者 · 导师：Zhongqiang Ren

In submission · ICRA 2027投稿中 · ICRA 2027

  • Idea. Select the horizon while respecting state, control, and obstacle constraints.
  • Validity. Across 100 problems from 10 systems, each run 5 times, replay pass rates are AL-HOP 100%, ALTRO 79.8%, DIRCOL/IPOPT 79.8%, and DIRCOL/SNOPT 70%.
  • Speed & quality. Up to 4× faster on average than common baselines, while finding trajectories with comparable costs.
  • 思路：在满足状态、控制与障碍约束的同时选择视界。
  • 有效性：10 类系统、100 个问题各运行 5 次，非线性回放通过率为 AL-HOP 100%，ALTRO 79.8%，DIRCOL/IPOPT 79.8%，DIRCOL/SNOPT 70%。
  • 速度与质量：相较常用基线，平均加速比最高达 4 倍，同时获得代价相当的轨迹。

Transferring Physical Supervision to Latent-Action VLA Pretraining

Miaomiao Dai · Second author · Title and author list withheld during double-blind review代淼淼 · 第二作者 · 双盲评审期间暂不公开标题与作者名单

Under double-blind review · ICLR 2027双盲评审中 · ICLR 2027

  • Motivation. Latent-action pretraining lets a VLA learn from video without action labels, but its targets come from visual change. Motions that look alike can hide different contact and force, so physical interaction is supervised only indirectly — and the sensors that measure it directly cover only a small slice of human and robot video.
  • Architecture. Three stages. A small set of human and robot interactions recorded with pressure, tactile, and torque sensors defines shared physical targets; a transfer model infers those targets from RGB for ordinary human and robot video; during pretraining, visual prediction and the inferred physical targets supervise the same latent actions. The deployed policy needs no tactile or torque sensing.
  • Evidence. Outperforms VLA-JEPA, the strongest latent-action baseline tested, on LIBERO and on every SimplerEnv task group; on five real-world manipulation tasks it beats both VLA-JEPA and π0.5 on every task.
  • 动机：隐动作预训练让 VLA 能从没有动作标签的视频里学习，但监督信号来自画面变化：看起来相似的动作，接触与受力可能完全不同，物理交互因此只被间接约束；而能直接测量它的传感器，只覆盖了人类与机器人视频里很小的一部分。
  • 架构：分三步。先用少量带压力、触觉、力矩传感器的人类与机器人交互，定义共享的物理目标；再由迁移模型只凭 RGB 推断这些目标，覆盖普通的人类与机器人视频；预训练时，视觉预测与推断出的物理目标共同监督同一组隐动作。部署时不需要任何触觉或力矩传感。
  • 证据：在 LIBERO 以及 SimplerEnv 的全部任务组上超过所比较的最强隐动作基线 VLA-JEPA；五个真实世界操作任务上，每一项都高于 VLA-JEPA 与 π0.5。

§03Proposal Ideas研究构想

Flow2Funnel: Feedback-Complete Action Heads for Vision–Language–Action Policies

Personal research idea · 2026 — 个人研究构想 · 2026 —

  • Question. When replanning is costly, can feedback within an action chunk improve disturbance recovery at the same VLA planning budget?
  • Approach. Learn the local response around a frozen VLA’s sampled plan, then use Riccati recursion to build feedback gains and a model-based recovery region.
  • Research plan. Test when local correction helps, when to request a new plan, and how these decisions hold up with deployment-available observations and realistic latency.
  • 问题：当重规划有明显代价时，动作块内部的反馈能否在相同 VLA 规划预算下提高扰动恢复能力？
  • 做法：围绕冻结 VLA 采样出的计划学习局部动态响应，通过 Riccati 递推构造反馈增益与基于模型的恢复区域。
  • 研究计划：检验何时局部校正有效、何时应重新规划，并在部署可得观测与真实推理延迟下验证这些判断。
