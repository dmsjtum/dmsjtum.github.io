Measure the Value, Learn the Slope值靠测，斜率靠学

When the world quietly changes, a learned world model’s predictions drift, but its sense of direction holds. So let the real world give the values and the model give the slopes: repeat the same action, measure where it really ends, and correct it along the model’s slope. Re-run once on fresh data with everything frozen, this held across three kinds of world models (our pixel models, the public DINO-WM, TD-MPC2). On the LeWM community benchmark it beats doing nothing in 5 of 7 pre-registered settings, for example lifting a frozen action chunk’s success from 14% to 93% on Reacher after a hidden motor change. It also repairs plans made by the world model itself: with no hidden change (PushT 62% → 92%) and with an 8× heavier block (26% → 54%). It works when a hidden change shifts the outcome but leaves the direction in place; when contact turns the direction, the robot has to probe for real. Agents that re-plan at every step mostly absorb simple biases anyway (TD-MPC2, DreamerV3). But large world models plan slowly and are usually re-planned only every few blocks; between re-plans, the same one-step correction from the measured current frame raised success on all four LeWM tasks (pre-registered on fresh starts), and a mechanism test showed the gain needs both the measurement and the model’s slope.现实条件悄悄变化时，学到的世界模型的预测值会跑偏，但它的方向感还在。所以让真实世界给数值、让模型给斜率：同一个动作重复做，实测它实际落在哪，再沿模型的斜率修正。在从没用过的新数据上、所有设置冻结后重跑一遍，这在三类世界模型上都成立（我们的像素模型、公开的 DINO-WM、TD-MPC2）。在 LeWM 社区基准上，它在 7 个预登记设定中的 5 个显著胜过不纠偏，例如电机悄悄变化后，Reacher 上冻结动作块的成功率从 14% 升到 93%。它还能修好世界模型自己做出的规划：没有隐藏变化时（PushT 62% → 92%），方块重 8 倍时（26% → 54%）也行。前提是隐藏变化只把结果推偏、不改方向；接触把方向转走时，只能靠真实试探。每一步都按实测重新规划的智能体，本来就能吸收简单的偏差（TD-MPC2、DreamerV3 上都核对过）；但大型世界模型规划很慢，通常每隔几个块才重新规划一次——在两次规划之间，从实测的当前画面出发做同样的一步修正，LeWM 四个任务的成功率都提高了（在全新起点上预登记检验）；机制检验表明，收益既要实测、也要模型的斜率。

When it works什么时候管用

direction stays方向不变The change shifts the outcome变化只把结果推偏

A heavier puck, a slipperier table or a weaker motor moves where the outcome lands, but not which way each part of the action pushes it. The model’s slope still points the right way, the measurement says how far off the try was, and practice closes most of the gap in five tries.圆盘更重、桌面更滑、电机更弱，都会让结果落到别处，但动作的每一部分把结果往哪边推并没有变。模型的斜率仍然指对方向，实测告诉它差了多少，练五次就补上大半差距。

Our pixel models (PushT, puck): five tries end at 0.26–0.48 of the uncorrected miss. Under heavier puck damping the true slope barely turns (cosine 0.84 with the normal one) and the model’s slope still matches it (0.83).我们的像素模型（PushT、圆盘）：练五次后误差降到不纠偏的 0.26–0.48。圆盘阻尼加大后，真实斜率几乎不转（与正常时余弦 0.84），模型斜率仍与它一致（0.83）。

direction turns方向被转走The change alters the contact变化改变了接触

When a finger turns a spinner and its motor gets stronger, it meets the spinner differently: the action that used to turn the spinner one way now turns it another. A model of the normal physics knows the old response well, but that response no longer holds, so no direction taken from it helps. Here the robot has to probe for real.手指拨转盘时，电机一旦变强，碰到转盘的方式就变了：原来把转盘往一边拨的动作，现在拨向了别处。按正常物理训练的模型很熟悉原来的响应，可那个响应已经不成立，所以从它取的方向都帮不上忙。这时只能真实地试探。

Finger and spinner, motor ×1.5: the model matches the normal response (correlation 0.69), but the changed response correlates only 0.18 with the normal one (0.07 at ×2.0). Five tries of practice end at 70 mm, against 45 mm for model-free probing.手指拨转盘，电机 ×1.5：模型与正常响应吻合（相关 0.69），但变化后的响应与正常响应只相关 0.18（×2.0 时 0.07）。练五次后误差 70 mm，无模型试探为 45 mm。

The first try alone does not yet tell the two cases apart reliably. Training the physics knobs to also match how the outcome responds to the action did not help either: the knobs then fit the response on the physics they were trained on, but not on new physics, and practice with them still loses to model-free probing (66 against 45 mm).仅凭第一次尝试，目前还不能可靠地分辨这两种情形。训练物理旋钮时再直接对准“动作→结果”的响应，也没有帮上忙：旋钮能拟合训练时见过的物理，却推广不到新的物理，用它练习仍输给无模型试探（66 对 45 mm）。

Inside closed-loop control. When a planner re-plans at every step from what the camera sees, it already absorbs simple hidden biases: on the LeWM benchmark, an actuator bias of up to 40% of the action half-range cost the standard sampling planner only 11–22 points of success, and TD-MPC2 and DreamerV3 behave the same way. Planning with a large world model is slow, though, so it is usually re-planned only every few blocks; LeWM’s own evaluation loop re-plans every five. Between re-plans we apply the same one-step correction, starting from the measured current frame, to the blocks still stored. Pre-registered on fresh starts, this raised success on all four LeWM tasks: by 12 points on Reacher and PushT (Reacher with doubled joint damping: 13 → 43 of 64 starts) and by 5.5 points on TwoRoom and Cube (15 starts better, 1 worse), at 1.1–1.2× the wall time of the authors’ loop. The gain is largest on Reacher and small on PushT. A mechanism test showed what carries it: starting the step from the model’s own predicted state instead of the measured one removes the gain (9.0 points lower), and so does replacing the model’s slope by a random matrix with the same spectrum (9.8 points lower); re-optimizing the plan in imagination alone does nothing. Re-run once more on untouched starts with everything frozen, the gain and its mechanism held: averaged over the four tasks, +11.8 points over the authors’ loop, +10.5 over starting from the predicted state, and +12.2 over a random slope. So: across tries, practice repairs actions that must run open loop; within an episode, the same step keeps a plan on course between expensive re-plans.放进闭环控制之后。规划器若每一步都按相机看到的重新规划，本身就能吸收简单的隐藏偏差：在 LeWM 基准上，执行器偏差加到动作半量程的 40%，标准采样规划器成功率只降 11–22 个百分点；TD-MPC2、DreamerV3 也是如此。但大型世界模型规划很慢，通常只能每隔几个块重新规划一次——LeWM 作者自己的评测循环就是每 5 块一次。我们在两次规划之间，从实测的当前画面出发，对还存着的后续动作块做同样的一步修正。在全新起点上预登记检验，LeWM 四个任务的成功率都提高了：Reacher、PushT 提高 12 个百分点（Reacher 关节阻尼加倍时，64 个起点里从 13 个升到 43 个），TwoRoom、Cube 提高 5.5 个百分点（15 个起点变好、1 个变差），墙钟时间是作者循环的 1.1–1.2 倍。收益在 Reacher 上最大，在 PushT 上很小。机制检验说明收益从哪来：把修正的起点换成模型自己预测的当前状态，收益消失（低 9.0 个百分点）；把模型斜率换成谱相同的随机矩阵，收益也消失（低 9.8 个百分点）；只在想象里重新优化计划没有用。在从没用过的起点上、所有设置冻结后再跑一遍，收益和机制都成立：四个任务平均，比作者循环高 11.8 个百分点，比从预测状态出发高 10.5，比随机斜率高 12.2。所以：跨次练习修正必须开环执行的动作；单次执行之内，同一步修正能在两次昂贵的规划之间把计划拉回正轨。

Result · slopes vs values结果 · 斜率与预测值

Measuring and following the slope beats planning on the same model’s predictions in all 10 settings, and planning is worse than doing nothing in 8 of 10. A fair test gives the planner the measurements too: then the two are about as accurate (8 settings, each side keeping its best try: 3 better, 2 worse, 3 ties), but the planner needs 100–1000× more model evaluations.在全部 10 组设定里，实测并沿斜率修正都胜过用同一模型的预测值规划；其中 8 组里，这样规划比什么都不做还差。公平对照是让规划器也拿到实测：这时两者精度大致相当（8 组，双方都取各自最好的一次：3 胜、2 负、3 平），但规划器要多花 100–1000 倍的模型计算。

What we learned我们学到的

A world model cannot see a hidden change; a measurement can. Planning on predicted values never reliably beat doing nothing under a hidden change (8 of 8 settings), and more compute did not help.世界模型看不见隐藏的变化，实测能看见。在隐藏变化下，用预测值规划从未可靠地胜过什么都不做（8 组里 8 组），加算力也没用。

Agents that re-plan at every step already absorb these changes. In a screen of TD-MPC2’s public multi-task model (69 task–change settings in Meta-World and DMControl, 16 development starts each), re-planning at every step succeeded on 63% of starts, practice on the frozen chunk on 22%, and the frozen chunk alone on 14%. On DreamerV3 (reaching), acting closed loop once matched five practice tries. Practice is for chunks that must run open loop.每一步都重新规划的智能体，自己就能吸收这些变化。在 TD-MPC2 公开多任务模型上的筛查中（Meta-World 与 DMControl 共 69 个"任务 × 变化"设定，每个 16 个开发起点），每步重新规划的成功率为 63%，对冻结动作块练习为 22%，冻结动作块原样执行为 14%。在 DreamerV3（到达任务）上，闭环执行一次就与练习五次相当。练习适用于必须开环执行的动作块。

Measure what you can; let the model guess only what you cannot; always take the direction from the model’s slope. The same one-step update works under two feedback timings. Across tries the whole miss is measured. Within an episode only the current state is; the rest of the path is still predicted, so part of the model’s bias remains (a two-block example shows that a re-anchored step can even make a correct plan worse; on LeWM it helps on average). Retraining the predictor at test time does not replace this: training LeWM’s predictor on the new physics for 100 episodes (the JEPA-TTT recipe) cut its prediction error by about 30% but did not raise success, while one slope step per block did (exploratory).能量到的差距用量的，量不到的才让模型猜，往哪儿改永远问模型的斜率。同一步更新有两种反馈时序：跨次练习时，整个偏差都是实测的；单次执行之内，只有当前状态是实测的，剩下的路径仍靠预测，模型的一部分偏差还在（一个两块的例子说明，重新锚定后的一步甚至可能把正确的计划改错；在 LeWM 上平均是有益的）。测试时重新训练预测器也替代不了它：按 JEPA-TTT 的配方在新物理下训练 LeWM 的预测器 100 个回合，预测误差降了约 30%，成功率却没有提高；每块一次斜率修正则提高了（探索性）。

Given the measurement, one derivative per try is enough. It is about as accurate as planning that also uses the measurement, with 100–1000× fewer model evaluations.有了实测，每次尝试求一次导就够了。精度与同样用上实测的规划大致相当，模型计算少 100–1000 倍。

Learn slopes by differentiating one latent dynamics model, not by predicting Jacobians: cosine with finite differences 0.70–0.77 vs 0.21. Camera augmentation keeps them accurate under image noise (0.69–0.70, vs 0.24–0.27 without it). Slope quality varies across world models: cosine 0.93 for the public TD-MPC2 model on a reaching task, 0.70–0.77 for our pixel model, 0.35 for public DINO-WM on PushT.斜率要对一个潜空间动力学模型求导，而不是直接预测雅可比：与有限差分的余弦为 0.70–0.77，直接预测只有 0.21。训练时加入相机增强，图像有噪声时斜率仍然准确（0.69–0.70，不加增强为 0.24–0.27）。不同世界模型的斜率质量差别很大：公开 TD-MPC2 模型在到达任务上余弦 0.93，我们的像素模型 0.70–0.77，公开 DINO-WM 在 PushT 上 0.35。

A slope only needs to be roughly right, because the value is measured: on unseen objects, a cosine of about 0.3 still halves the error (0.50); model-free search leaves 0.84–0.96 after 5 tries.斜率只需大致正确，因为偏差是实测的：在未见过的物体上，余弦约 0.3 也能把误差减半（0.50）；无模型搜索试 5 次后还剩 0.84–0.96。

Honest scope: it works for repeatable situations with systematic error, when the hidden change leaves the direction in place. With a stochastic diffusion policy, most failures are sampling luck, so practice comes after re-rolling.适用范围要说实话：它适用于可重复、误差系统性、且隐藏变化不改方向的情形。随机扩散策略的失败多半是采样运气，所以先重来，再练习。

In two screening studies on a frozen LeRobot diffusion policy (PushT simulation), re-rolling (resampling the policy’s noise and keeping the best measured attempt) rescued 56–100% of failed episodes within 10 executions and beat slope-based refinement. In the second study, only 10 of 96 failures under a repeated push were systematic (no success in 24 re-rolls); none of the 32 failures in either of the other two settings. In a third study, a hidden payload that shifts the T’s centre of mass by 30% of its bar made 30 of 64 failures systematic. On those, correcting only the last 32 steps rescued 3% with our model’s slope, 10% with the best model-free update, and only 17% even with the simulator’s true slope: an error built up over the whole episode cannot be undone at the end.在对冻结的 LeRobot 扩散策略（PushT 仿真）做的两轮筛查中，重来（换一组策略采样噪声，保留实测最好的一次）在 10 次执行内救回了 56–100% 的失败回合，并且胜过基于斜率的精修。第二轮中，重复推扰动下的 96 次失败里只有 10 次是系统性的（重来 24 次都没成功）；另外两种设定各 32 次失败，一次也不是。第三轮中，让 T 的质心沿横梁偏移 30% 的隐藏配重，使 64 次失败中的 30 次变成系统性的。在这些失败上只修正最后 32 步：用我们模型的斜率救回 3%，最好的无模型更新救回 10%，即使用仿真器的真斜率也只救回 17%——整局累积出来的误差，到末段再改已经来不及。

Where this sits: the principle, measuring the outcome and taking only the direction of the fix from an approximate model, is old: Abbeel, Quigley and Ng (ICML 2006), task-level iterative learning control (Atkeson and colleagues, most recently Suresh and Atkeson 2026), and learned delta dynamics for rope whipping (IRP, Chi et al. 2022). What we add is where the slope comes from, a pixel world model trained only to predict (ours and the public DINO-WM); a controlled test against planning with the same model’s values; and practice on a frozen generative policy’s action chunks, including where it fails. That prediction error is a poor guide to control is also known (Lambert et al. 2020; a 2026 control theory of predictability in latent world models).与前人的关系：这个原则本身——结果靠实测，只从近似模型里取修正方向——由来已久：Abbeel、Quigley 与 Ng（ICML 2006），任务级迭代学习控制（Atkeson 等，最近的是 Suresh 与 Atkeson 2026），以及为甩绳学习增量动力学的 IRP（Chi 等 2022）。我们补上的是斜率的来源——只为预测而训练的像素世界模型（我们的，以及公开的 DINO-WM）；与"用同一模型的预测值规划"做受控对照；以及在冻结生成式策略的动作块上练习，并如实给出它失效的地方。"预测误差不能指导控制"也已有人指出（Lambert 等 2020；2026 年一篇关于 latent 世界模型可预测性的控制理论工作）。

The look carries enough information: identifying the right physics from that one trajectory picks the best available action. But fine-tuning a learned world model on one trajectory mostly memorises it (it recovers 0–34% of the gap). On the same start, anchoring on the look already halves the error (puck 20 / 28 px against 39 / 52 without correction). Teaching the world model a few physics knobs works on the reaching arm: trained across many simulated motor gains and joint dampings, a model with 4 knobs is set by one look and then, in imagination, recovers 73–88% of the gap to the best available action (34 → 13 mm at gain ×1.2, 84 → 27 mm at ×1.5), also beyond its training range (153 → 75 mm at ×2.0); knobs set from a look at a different gain make things worse, as they should. Two honest limits from the next test: the knobs only recognise the kinds of change they were trained on (a change of a new kind, such as a constant torque bias, is not recognised), and one look plus one execution is worth roughly 1.5 to more than 5 real practice tries, depending on the change (gain ×1.5: 46 mm after one look, 50 mm after two tries, 25 mm after five). The two combine best: practising with the calibrated model ends 20–47% lower than practising with the original one (gain ×1.5: 25 → 15 mm; torque bias: 44 → 23 mm). On our pixel puck model the knobs do recognise the physics (the model’s predicted error drops from 49 to 22 px), but its own prediction errors still dominate the choice, so acting once from imagination is no better than not correcting (43 vs 39 px at damping ×2). There, measured practice stays the tool, and a look that sets the knobs does not make it converge further: the knobs correct the model’s predicted values, not its slopes, and on this bed even the simulator’s own slope gains little. A second test task with contact (a finger turning a spinner) shows where the method stops: there the model does not know which parts of the action turn the spinner. Neither its derivative nor finite trial changes made in imagination give a usable direction, practice with it loses to a model-free search that probes with real tries, and the knobs close only 12–18% of the gap. The reason is physical: the model knows how the spinner responds under the normal physics, but the hidden change itself turns that response around (the stronger the change, the less the changed response resembles the normal one), so no direction taken from a model of the normal physics can help, and real probing is needed.
