Measure the Value, Learn the Slope值靠测，斜率靠学

When the world quietly changes, a world model’s predictions go wrong, but its sense of direction survives. So the camera gives the values and the model gives only the direction: measure where the action really ended, then correct it along the model’s slope.现实悄悄变化时，世界模型的预测会出错，但它的方向感还在。所以让相机给数值、让模型只给方向：实测动作实际落在哪，再沿模型的斜率修正。

Idea思路

usual常见做法Predict, then correct先预测，再纠偏

ours我们Try, measure, correct先试，量出来，再改

Slopes, not values.取斜率，不取预测值。

Where it fits适用在哪

Repeated actions. Measure the miss, step along the slope, try again: a few tries fix it.可重复的动作。测出偏差，沿斜率修正，再试一次：几次就能修好。

Slow planners. A large world model plans too slowly to re-plan every step (DINO-WM: 4–12 s per plan), so it re-plans only every few blocks, or just once. One slope step from the current frame (0.15 s) fills the gap: on DINO-WM with a single plan, success rose from 57% to 77%. Re-planning every block instead took 2.7× longer and was not better (LeWM).慢的规划器。大型世界模型规划太慢（DINO-WM 一次 4–12 秒），没法每步重新规划，只能隔几块规划一次，甚至只规划一次。用当前画面沿斜率修正一步（0.15 秒）填上中间的空档：DINO-WM 只规划一次时，成功率从 57% 升到 77%。改成每块都重新规划，耗时 2.7 倍，效果也不更好（LeWM）。

Not needed, or not useful. Agents that already decide cheaply at every step (TD-MPC2, DreamerV3) absorb the change themselves. When contact turns the action’s effect, the model’s direction is wrong.用不着或用不上。每步都能便宜地重新决策的智能体（TD-MPC2、DreamerV3）自己就吸收了变化；接触让动作的效果转了方向时，模型的方向是错的。
