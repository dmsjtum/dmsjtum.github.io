Measure the Value, Learn the Slope值靠测，斜率靠学

When the world quietly changes, a world model’s predicted landing point goes wrong, but its slope, its sense of direction, survives. So the camera measures the value, where the action really landed, and the model gives only the slope: measure the miss, then correct the action along the slope.现实悄悄变化时，世界模型预测的落点会出错，但它的斜率（方向感）还在。所以数值（落点）交给相机实测，模型只给斜率：量出差了多少，再沿斜率修正动作。

Results · slopes vs predicted values结果 · 斜率对比预测值

In all 10 settings, measuring the value and correcting along the model’s slope beats planning on the same model’s predicted values; planning on predictions ends worse than doing nothing in 8 of 10.10 组设定全部如此：实测数值（落点）、再沿模型的斜率（方向感）修正，胜过用同一个模型的预测值去规划；按预测值规划有 8 组比什么都不做还差。

128 fresh starts per row, re-run with everything frozen. Each change was sized on other starts to at least halve the recorded chunk’s success. A gain scales every motor command (×0.8: the motor is 20% weaker); an offset adds a constant push to every command, a share of the largest command, in a random direction. Ours counts the fifth try. The last column is the fair test: after each try, the gap between where the action really landed and where the model predicted is added to the model, and the authors’ planner plans again from scratch; its best of five tries counts. It is beaten in 3 of 7 rows and about level in the rest, at about 190× our model computation. Not significant: with 128 starts the gain could be chance (paired 95% lower bound not above 0).每行 128 个从没用过的起点，所有设置冻结后重跑。每种变化的幅度事先在别的起点上定好，使录下的动作块成功率至少减半。增益把每个电机指令按比例缩小（×0.8：电机弱了 20%）；偏置给每个指令加一个恒定的推力，大小按最大指令的百分比，方向随机。我们按第 5 次计。最后一列是公平对照：每次执行后，把实测落点与模型预测落点之差补进模型，再让作者的规划器从头规划，5 次里取最好的一次；它有 3 行明显不如我们，其余大致持平，模型计算却是我们的约 190 倍。不显著：128 个起点下，这点提升可能只是运气（配对 95% 下界不大于 0）。

Where it fits适用在哪

Repeated actions. Measure the miss, step along the slope, try again: a few tries fix it.可重复的动作。测出偏差，沿斜率修正，再试一次：几次就能修好。

Slow planners. A large world model plans too slowly to re-plan every step (DINO-WM: 4–12 s per plan), so it re-plans only every few blocks, or just once. One slope step from the current frame (0.15 s) fills the gap: success rose from 62% to 75% over four LeWM tasks, and from 57% to 77% on DINO-WM with a single plan. Re-planning every block instead took 2.7× longer and was not better (LeWM).慢的规划器。大型世界模型规划太慢（DINO-WM 一次 4–12 秒），没法每步重新规划，只能隔几块规划一次，甚至只规划一次。用当前画面沿斜率修正一步（0.15 秒）填上中间的空档：LeWM 四个任务的成功率从 62% 升到 75%，DINO-WM 只规划一次时从 57% 升到 77%。改成每块都重新规划，耗时 2.7 倍，效果也不更好（LeWM）。

Not needed, or not useful. Agents that already decide cheaply at every step (TD-MPC2, DreamerV3) absorb the change themselves. When contact turns the action’s effect, the model’s direction is wrong.用不着或用不上。每步都能便宜地重新决策的智能体（TD-MPC2、DreamerV3）自己就吸收了变化；接触让动作的效果转了方向时，模型的方向是错的。
