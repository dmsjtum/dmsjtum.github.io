Research

§01Planning

  • HOP: Fast Differential Dynamic Programming for Horizon-Optimal Trajectory Planning

    Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) · 2026

    A closed-form result that scores every candidate time horizon in one backward sweep, turning horizon search from O(N²) into O(N) — up to 40× faster than brute force, with the same selected horizon and cost.

  • AL-HOP: Augmented-Lagrangian Horizon-Optimal Planning for Fast Constrained Trajectory Optimization

    Under review · 2026

    Extends HOP to hard state and control constraints — obstacles, actuator limits — with an augmented-Lagrangian formulation that preserves the one-sweep horizon prediction. Produced valid solutions on 100/100 benchmark problems across 10 dynamical systems.

§02Manipulation

  • A Voice-Interactive Closed-Loop VLA System for Low-Cost Tabletop Manipulation

    B.E. thesis, Shanghai Jiao Tong University · 2026

    Spoken commands drive a π0.5 policy on an SO-101 arm, with a vision-language model verifying that the objects you named are actually in view before anything moves. Led a team of five; 70% end-to-end success with zero safety incidents.
